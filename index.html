Our Story

Three generations on the same ground.

We've been working this land in Cumming, Georgia since 1969 — raising our family and our animals side by side. It's not fancy. It's honest.

Every cow has a name. Every calf gets met the day it's born. This site is where we share what life on the farm really looks like — the good days, the muddy ones, and everything in between.
